Identify carers
Recognise and respond to growing numbers of carers
There are more carers than ever. Drought, bushfire, flooding, economic insecurity and housing stress mean more people in our community are experiencing mental health challenges and distress, leading more people into caring roles. The Government must identify communities where need is growing and build targeted supports.

Support carers
Build integrated and carer-specific services
Carers need tailored supports that reflect their diverse needs, not only within mental health services. Government must invest in health and human services that recognise carers and extend funding for initiatives like Mental Health and Wellbeing Connect centres.
Learn more
Involve carers
Include carer voices in system design
Mental health carers drive change and support communities often without recognition. Government commitments to centre lived experience must be honoured by embedding carer perspectives across system design and leadership.
